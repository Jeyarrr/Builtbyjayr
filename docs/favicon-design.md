# Personal avatar favicon

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using `public/jayr.png` as the appearance reference. Exported to PNG favicon sizes and a multi-resolution ICO. No letters or monogram.

Final assets: `public/jayr-avatar.png` (512px), `public/favicon-avatar-16.png`, `public/favicon-avatar-32.png`, `public/favicon-avatar-48.png`, `public/favicon.ico` (16/32/48px), and `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180px).

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: personal developer portfolio browser favicon. Input image: reference photograph of Jayr Casano, use this man's appearance for the icon. Create one square, crisp, flat illustrated avatar icon of his face, recognizable from the photograph: short black side-swept hair, broad softly rounded face, medium warm skin tone, straight dark eyebrows, no glasses, clean-shaven with only subtle natural facial shading. Tight face-dominant head-and-tiny-shoulders crop, centered face fills most of the square; small hint of navy blazer and white shirt at bottom, no arms or torso. Bold simplified shapes and a few confident dark outlines, friendly composed expression, high contrast and minimal detail readable at 16px and 32px. Pale lime green #c8ef91 solid background matches the BuiltByJayr portfolio. Small safe margin around head, square canvas. No letters, no text, no monogram, no symbols, no watermarks, no mockup, no grid, exactly one finished avatar. This is a miniature identity icon, not a realistic photo or elaborate illustration.
